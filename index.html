Hermes Personal

Hermes Personal is a private, single-user assistant run by its developer on his own computer. It is not offered to the public, and only the developer's own Google accounts are connected to it.

Hermes Personal は、開発者本人が自分のPC上で動かす個人用アシスタントです。一般には提供しておらず、接続するのは開発者本人のGoogleアカウントだけです。

Privacy Policy / プライバシーポリシー

Last updated: 2026-10-23

Data accessed

  • Gmail: read-only (gmail.readonly) — to summarize the owner's own messages for the owner.
  • Google Calendar: read-only (calendar.readonly) — to list the owner's own meetings for the owner.

How data is used

  • Data is used only to prepare summaries and reminders for the account owner.
  • The app does not send, delete, or modify email or calendar events.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • OAuth tokens and data are stored only on the owner's computer. Content may be processed by the AI model provider configured by the owner solely to produce those summaries.

Limited Use

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
